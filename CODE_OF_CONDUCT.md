# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to making participation in SpectraCast welcoming, respectful,
and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience, background,
identity, or device platform.

Expected behavior includes constructive feedback, clear technical discussion,
respect for privacy and security, and assuming good intent while resolving
disagreements.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
sexualized conduct, malicious disclosure of private data, and deliberate
disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers. Reports will
be reviewed and handled fairly. Maintainers may remove comments, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe.
